Privacy policy

How Zine accesses and handles your information.

Effective September 10, 2026

Who operates Zine

Zine is operated by Erik Johansson as a personal reading app for himself and invited friends. For privacy questions or data requests, email ejohane@gmail.com.

Information you provide

Zine processes account information used to sign you in, links you save, tags and collections you create, reading progress, and your source preferences. It stores imported content and metadata so you can use your inbox and library.

Connected accounts and Google data

Connecting a service is optional and requires your authorization. Google connections provide account identity information, such as your email address and profile, to identify the account you connect.

  • YouTube: Zine reads your subscriptions and channel and video information to bring videos into your feed.
  • Gmail: Zine reads message information, including senders, headers, subjects, snippets, and message content, to identify and import newsletters. The Gmail read permission applies to email generally; it is not a Google-enforced newsletter-only permission.
  • Spotify: Zine reads saved podcast shows and related episode information.

Zine stores access and refresh tokens encrypted on its backend so connections can continue working between visits. The Google permissions used for these reading features do not grant Zine permission to send email or edit your YouTube subscriptions.

How information is used and processed

Information is used to synchronize your selected sources, display and organize content, track reading, maintain connections, and troubleshoot the service. Saved or imported content and metadata may be processed by automated features that generate summaries, classifications, tags, and search representations.

Cloudflare provides backend hosting, storage, and Workers AI processing. Clerk provides Zine account authentication. These providers process information needed to supply those services. Opening an original article, video, or other external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Zine does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google user data is used to provide and improve the reading features you use, not to train generalized AI models. Zine’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Account information, imported content, reading activity, and connection metadata are retained to operate your account and library. Technical logs may contain request identifiers, operational events, and error information used to diagnose problems.

Disconnecting an account removes the connection from Zine and stops future authorized synchronization. Content previously imported or saved can remain in your library. Disconnecting is not a request to erase all previously collected data.

Your controls and deletion requests

You can manage or disconnect sources in Settings → Sources. You can also revoke Google access through your Google Account connections.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your Zine data, contact ejohane@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. We will confirm the request and explain the deletion scope, including any retained backups or records that cannot be removed immediately.

Changes and questions

This page will be updated when Zine’s data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Contact Erik at the email above with questions before connecting an account.
