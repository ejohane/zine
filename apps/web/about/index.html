A library you can return to

Keep the pieces you want to revisit, organize them with tags and collections, and track your reading. Automated summaries and content suggestions help you navigate what you have saved.

You choose what to connect

Google connections are optional. Zine requests read access to YouTube subscriptions and Gmail messages to provide its video and newsletter features. Gmail’s permission covers reading email; Google does not limit that permission to newsletters alone.

Manage connected accounts in Settings → Sources. Disconnecting stops future authorized access; it is separate from deleting content already imported into Zine. Read our privacy policy for the details.
